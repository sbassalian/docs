Growi's MCP server lives at `POST https://api.growi.io/mcp`. Clients list its tools
when they connect, so you never call these by hand. This page tells you what
Claude can look up and change for you. To connect, see
[Connect Claude to Growi](/essentials/connect-claude).

## How it works

- **Transport.** Stateless Streamable HTTP: one JSON-RPC request per `POST`, plain JSON
  responses, no sessions. `GET /mcp` returns `405`.
- **Auth.** A Growi API key as a bearer token, or a sign-in through claude.ai or
  ChatGPT (OAuth). What a key can do follows the role of the person it belongs to,
  checked again on every call. A viewer key never writes.
- **Scope.** One organization, plus its child brands for a parent organization,
  the same as the REST API.
- **Limits.** 60 units a minute and 1,000 an hour per key, separate from the REST API.
  A read costs 1 unit, a change 3 and a heavy report 5.
- **Formats.** Money is integer cents in your organization's currency (`*_cents`),
  dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, and list tools take `page` and `per_page` (up to 100) and
  return `meta.has_more`.

## Reads, for every role

| Tool | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `get_organization` | Your brands and connected sales channels. Claude calls it first |
| `list_campaigns` | Campaigns, contracts and programs with status, dates and creator counts |
| `get_campaign` | One campaign in detail: tiers, products and creators by status |
| `list_campaign_creators` | A campaign's creators, filtered by status or searched by name or email |
| `get_creator` | One creator's lifetime orders, posts and payout totals |
| `list_orders` | Attributed orders across TikTok Shop, Shopify, Amazon, iOS and Stripe |
| `get_sales_timeseries` | Daily revenue and funnel events for the organization, a campaign or a creator (up to 92 days) |
| `list_creator_payouts` | Payouts with status, amounts and due dates |
| `list_invoices` | Your invoices with status and totals |
| `list_posts` | Creator content with lifetime engagement |
| `get_top_posts` | Top posts by views for a period (up to 92 days, top 50) |
| `list_products` | Your catalog across channels |
| `list_creator_performance` | Per-creator tier, samples, approved videos, TikTok Shop GMV and contact details |
| `list_delivered_videos` | Approved delivered videos with link, posted date, creator and campaign |
| `list_sample_applications` | Shopify sample applications and claimed gifts |
| `summarize_view_bonuses` | View bonuses per post by tier, per payout cycle, with a CSV link. Heavy |
| `get_growi_guide` | How Growi works, for "how" and "why" questions |
| `explain_creator_payout` | Why a creator was paid what they were, line by line, with a CSV link. Heavy |
| `get_creator_attribution_breakdown` | Where a creator's attributed sales came from |
| `get_creator_post_count_audit` | Which of a creator's posts counted, and why others didn't |
| `diagnose_campaign_video_attribution` | Why a video did or didn't count toward a campaign |
| `diagnose_creator_discount_code` | Why a creator's discount code isn't working |
| `list_tts_top_creators`, `list_tts_top_videos`, `list_tts_shop_videos_by_views`, `get_tts_revenue_trend` | TikTok Shop top creators, top videos and revenue trend |

## Dashboard numbers, for every role

These read the same numbers as the dashboard pages, so Claude's answers match what you
see. Tools marked heavy cost 5 units.

| Tool | Page | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `get_content_summary` | Home | Views and engagement gained against the previous period, and active posts, creators and accounts. Heavy |
| `get_content_series` | Home | The daily series for your organization or campaigns, or one post (`social_post_id`). Pass `group` (`creator`, `post`, `platform` or `campaign`) to split it into the top groups plus everyone else. Heavy |
| `list_top_content` | Home | Leaderboards of posts, creators, accounts, campaigns and platforms. Heavy |
| `get_content_filters` | Home | The campaigns and platforms you can filter by |
| `get_creator_activity` | Home | New creators, posts per day, retention and, for agencies, each brand's progress. Heavy |
| `get_content_spikes` | Home | Days that jumped to at least 3 times the trailing 14-day median, each with its top 3 posts. Heavy |
| `explain_day` | Home | One day broken down: total against normal, top posts, everyone else, and a note when post readings were low, plus a one-line summary. Heavy |
| `list_breakout_posts` | Home | Posts gaining at least 5 times their own 7-day pace right now, with the 24-hour gain, the pace ratio and a sparkline. Heavy |
| `get_stores_overview` | Stores › Overview | GMV, commission and orders per store and blended, against the previous period. Heavy |
| `get_shopify_stats` | Shopify | Ambassador totals, series, top creators and top products. Heavy |
| `get_meta_ads_overview` | Meta Ads | Overview, over time, by creator and top creatives. Heavy |
| `get_tiktok_shop_stats` | TikTok Shop | Outreach scorecard totals for the window |
| `get_payout_totals` | Payouts | Owed and paid totals and what's owed per cycle. `null` while a payout recalculates. Heavy |
| `get_payout_breakdown` | Payouts | One payout's breakdown: components, tier lines, views paid for and top posts. Heavy |

Try "Why did views jump on October 12?", "Which posts are breaking out right now?" or
"Show views per day split by creator for the Spring campaign."

## Changes any signed-in role can make

These need a key tied to a person (any key created while signed in, or a claude.ai sign-in).

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `repair_creator_discount_code` | Fixes a creator's broken discount code |
| `update_campaign_discount` | Changes a campaign's discount |
| `assign_creator_discount_code` | Gives a creator a specific code |
| `get_invoice_pdf` | Gets an invoice PDF |

## Owners and admins only

**Customer data**, masked: `list_creator_sales`, `get_order_journey`,
`list_creator_referrers`, `search_shopify_schema`, `query_shopify_admin` (read-only
Shopify queries) and `get_organization_balance`.

**Fraud checks**: `get_shopify_fraud_report`, `get_creator_fraud_dossier` and
`generate_shopify_fraud_report`.

**Campaign and creator changes**: commission and tier rates, milestone and per-post
bonuses, payout delay, views payout, names and descriptions, contract templates,
tracking and attribution settings, social channels, Discord, content submissions,
free sample products, discount settings, approving or denying applications, adding
and removing creators, attributing videos, refunding your remaining balance and
changing an invoice's payment method.

**Meta ads**: `create_meta_ads` and `manage_meta_ads` (launch or pause). Listing ad
sources, builder options and ads works for every role. See
[building Meta ads](/essentials/connect-claude#4-building-meta-ads).

Claude previews every change before making it. Changes that move money or remove
data need a confirmation that works once and expires after 10 minutes. Every change
is logged with who asked for it.
