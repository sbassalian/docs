<Note>
  Each payout has a `state`: `ready`, `canceled`, or `updating` while it
  recalculates. An `updating` payout has `pending_update: true` and `null`
  amounts, dates, views and content counts until it's ready. While payouts are
  being recalculated for your whole organization, every row comes back `updating`
  with null amounts. Call again in a few minutes.
</Note>
