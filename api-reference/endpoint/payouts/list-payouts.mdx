<Tip>
  Replaces [`/stats/creator_payouts`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-creator-payouts), which is deprecated.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Each payout has a `state`: `ready`, `canceled`, or `updating` while it
  recalculates. An `updating` payout has `pending_update: true` and `null`
  amounts, dates, views and content counts until it's ready. While payouts are
  being recalculated for your whole organization, every row comes back `updating`
  with null amounts. Call again in a few minutes.
</Note>
<Note>
  `amount_cents` is what the payout earns as currently calculated. For a paid
  payout, read `paid_amount_cents` to get what was actually paid, together with
  `paid_at`. The two can differ when the brand set a custom amount or the payout
  was recalculated after payment. `paid_amount_cents` and `custom_amount` are
  returned even while a payout is `updating`.
</Note>
<Tip>
  To sync incrementally, pass `updated_since` with the time you started your
  previous call. You get back only the payouts whose status, invoice or
  calculated values changed since then. `cycle` is 1-based and matches each
  row's `cycle_number`.
</Tip>
